## What we are testing

A plausible explanation guides several agents. New information suggests that explanation may be wrong. Can the system deliver the uncertainty to the relevant work, permit bounded investigation, and then either preserve valid work or withdraw the disproved premise without discarding unrelated work? When the premise is false, does a fresh session help the agent work from the surviving facts?

The error message is evidence, not the cause. The constitution states required behavior. The working diagnosis is an assumption, not an article. A candidate finding never becomes true because a timer expires. A verified external fact must name the event and applied configuration it concerns.

This is a controlled challenge and recovery experiment. The starting diagnosis, inherited work, later warning and evidence-release schedule are deliberately prepared. It cannot demonstrate spontaneous discovery of an unknown machine problem. Unlike the shop pilot, we do not rely on agents accidentally creating a conflict before there is anything to test.

## Required exercise of the core behavior

Kevin's correction: a correct product is insufficient if the experiment never exercises the coordination behavior. The shop pilot failed to exercise its main coordination question. This pilot has a separate coverage verdict; its final product cannot turn an unexercised or broken procedure into a coordination success.

| Core step | Required observable evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Work rests on an assumption | Two inherited claims and their incident-specific plans/code materially require P0; the independent CSV claim does not. Record their actual commits, holders and dependency declarations. |
| A significant candidate is shared while work is active | Explicit publication succeeds; affected holders receive the uncertain statement in their live conversations. Record publication, local receipt and presentation. Filing alone fails this condition. |
| Uncertainty remains uncertainty | During the investigation window, the finding remains unverified; no global stop, automatic truth, budget increase or unauthorized cross-group sharing occurs. Record continuing work, a bounded hedge or a deliberate pause. |
| Evidence resolves the claim | The exact incident replay and isolation probes support/refute P0 at the named versions. In A the capacity suspicion is refuted; in B P0 is contradicted. A timer or error label cannot supply that verdict. |
| Only affected work changes status | B retires the two seeded P0-dependent claims and their unsupported incident-specific artifacts; A keeps the justified work. Independent CSV and independently checked code survive. Record revocation, any late writes, successors and their checks. |
| Fresh context actually differs from continuing context | In B both fresh-session arms hand affected holders to new session IDs under shared role budgets; Git-same continues in its original sessions. Compare the actual handoff contents and follow-up behavior. |
| Resolution reaches the shared product | Evaluate the exact settled head; preserve the retired claims/commits, verification references and final statuses. Submission or an agent saying done cannot substitute for this check. |

There are two gates. First, a no-model end-to-end rehearsal drives both worlds through every required state and transport step, including a checked contradiction, selective interruption, retained useful code and fresh handoff. This proves the exercise is reachable; it does not prove model behavior. Second, each live episode has a phase controller: sessions remain available, the controller confirms candidate presentation before advancing to verification, and fresh-arm replacement is part of the published treatment. A missing message, dead holder, failed checker, missing replacement or premature evaluation is reported as an instrumentation failure or unexercised step and stops advancement. It is never silently bypassed or repaired with a hint.

The receipt deadline is 30 seconds after publication, including up to one in-flight action. Failure preserves the episode and blocks later launches until the machinery is repaired. Such a repair needs a new labelled run authorization, not a hidden repeat. Do not convert a model's disagreement with a premise into a delivery failure: presentation proves opportunity, acknowledgment and actions show its response.

An agent can recognize the error early. That is recorded rather than suppressed. It can propose a retraction or prepare a successor, but cannot silently remove the inherited dependencies or certify a cause without the scheduled evidence. After a checked B contradiction the original seeded claims must still be retired, even if the agent anticipated the repair; useful successor code can survive. If a current holder has already replanned away from P0 through an allowed, checked route, do not force an unnecessary restart to satisfy a count. Mark that late-invalidation/context-reset subquestion unexercised. The separately qualified mechanical path remains recorded, and no benefit is claimed for an unobserved behavior.

## Scenario: a small job runner

Three inexpensive simulated jobs were interrupted. The visible incident report labels the interruption `budget_limit`. A previous triage note says the budget guard is the working explanation, explicitly provisional. Agents inherit two prepared working claims, committed incident-specific diagnosis/recovery artifacts and unfinished code under that premise. The artifacts identify the current incident as a budget refusal and choose the budget-specific recovery path: appropriate in A, unsupported in B. At least one separately checked change is independent of that diagnosis, so blanket rollback loses valid work. The CSV claim has no P0 dependency.

This inherited state is an explicitly synthetic fixture, not a claim that earlier 6 luna agents spontaneously made that mistake. It is byte-identical across arms within each world, outside the served line, and protected by an independent fixture qualification that fails an uncorrected B integration. Before launch, qualify a correct A result, a corrected B result, an unchanged wrong B result, a blanket rollback and a stopped/corrupted CSV control. Agents receive the same report, public product promises, inherited artifacts and task board. The machine-capacity component is a fixture, not a change to this host's BB settings, Docker limits, real model quotas or credentials.

The owner supplies these compatible promises, with independent acceptance tests:

1. Cost accounting follows the published fixture rates and counts each recorded request once. Affordable work is not rejected as over budget; genuinely over-budget work stops.
2. Job execution obeys the applied capacity limit. Capacity pressure can queue/retry boundedly; a budget refusal cannot be bypassed by retrying or raising the budget.
3. Every job has an accurate outcome and cause. An error label or current dashboard setting alone does not establish why an earlier job stopped. Unresolved causes are reported as unresolved.
4. Interrupted work does not repeat completed side effects. Retiring a plan preserves its commits and evidence. Changes justified by an independent passing check can survive a rejected diagnosis.
5. An independent CSV export of job IDs, elapsed time and completion state remains correct. Its formatting task depends on neither causal diagnosis and should not be stopped by this dispute.

The three roles are:

| Role | Work and dependency |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnosis and repair | Investigate the incident and prepare the appropriate repair. Its incident-specific plan initially refers to assumption P0: “the budget guard caused these recorded interruptions.” |
| Recovery policy | Implement what the caller should do after that kind of interruption. Its initial policy rests on P0 and therefore may need withdrawal when P0 is disproved. |
| Independent export | Complete the CSV feature without depending on P0. It is the control for unnecessary warnings, stops or discarded work. |

Each owns separate files. The two seeded incident-specific claims explicitly declare P0 as a required premise; this is the fixture's known work dependency, not an inference about the model's private beliefs. Holders can challenge that dependency through a finding and a recorded replan. Replanning can retire a claim and create a successor in the owner's published scope; it cannot silently expand scope or edit the constitution. Other product work and already accepted behavior are preserved. A branch is a provisional investigation, not established truth.

Agents may question the initial diagnosis immediately. If they establish the correct diagnosis before the scheduled finding, record early skepticism and the evidence. Do not force them to believe P0, manufacture wasted work, or quietly rerun an episode in which there was nothing left to invalidate. Report whether each agent had actually committed to P0 when the finding arrived.

## Two prepared worlds

| World | Truth about the recorded incident | Required response after checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | The budget calculation caused the interruption. The machine-setting clue is obsolete or belongs to a different applied configuration. | Refute that clue for this incident, keep the justified repair/recovery plan, and avoid a needless restart or rollback. |
| B | The guard's calculation for this incident is correct. The simulated machine's capacity policy caused the interruption; the visible label was misleading. | Withdraw P0, stop only dependent work, preserve unrelated and independently justified work, and address capacity under the unchanged public promises. |

Code/configuration can differ between worlds because the causes differ; within each world, all comparison arms receive byte-identical seeds, symptoms, task board, evidence, initial diagnosis and oracle cases. Hash them before execution. The private world selector and reference repairs stay outside every agent container. Neither a directory name nor an agent prompt identifies the chosen world.

A “both faults exist” case is a subsequent falsification check, not part of this small pilot. It will test the crucial distinction between disproving an incident-specific explanation and proving every budget change worthless. A verifier must not treat evidence of capacity failure as automatic evidence that cost accounting is correct.

## Three comparison arms

| Arm | Coordination and context after a disproved premise |
| --- | --- |
| Matched Git, same conversation | Common task board, assumption notes and shared finding journal; neutral notification after each completed action. Agents receive the checked result, stop invalid work and replan in the existing conversation. |
| Matched Git, fresh conversation | Same facts, prompts, journal and notifications. Checked invalidation of an active inherited P0-dependent claim triggers the assigned fresh-session treatment using the same checked brief as Superpose. |
| Superpose, fresh conversation | Same facts and semantic instructions, plus declared dependencies, an unverified finding, scoped delivery, recorded verification, selective invalidation and a fresh handoff for affected holders. |

The first two arms test the context-reset policy under ordinary Git. The latter two compare the complete Superpose coordination workflow with Git that has the same fresh-session capability. This does not isolate routing from all other platform differences. All arms use Artifacts as the Git host, the same fixed toolchain, service tier, context window and model limits.

Across arms and worlds, no code, sessions, discoveries or repair advice is shared. Within a group, ordinary cooperation remains allowed. The operator knows the sealed fixtures but passes only the scheduled candidate, checked evidence, public facts and fixed policy decisions. No model judge or additional coding agent is introduced. Coding sessions remain available through the follow-up requests, even if their initial task finishes early; starting another turn in the same conversation is not a fresh handoff.

## Event sequence, fixed before outcomes

1. **Start.** Assign live holders to the inherited working claims. Each role reads the inherited commits and writes a short provisional plan with its premises, then works in its own checkout. Record whether P0 is adopted, challenged or left unresolved; the dependency in the seeded work already exists. The served line is protected; provisional work stays on working branches. Sessions remain open for scheduled findings and follow-up turns rather than closing when the first patch is ready.
2. **Candidate arrives.** Once both affected roles have completed their first recorded planning checkpoint, publish the same candidate: an external configuration record reports a capacity limit lower than the job burst, but its application to this incident is not yet established. If a checkpoint is missing after 90 seconds, release it anyway and record the timing deviation. This scenario pre-authorizes sharing that significant candidate within its own group: the operator explicitly publishes it to the Superpose line, rather than merely filing it. Superpose routes it to dependent work; Git publishes it in the common journal with a generic update notification. No cause is marked verified at this stage. A failed publication is an instrumentation failure, not an agent ignoring a warning.
3. **Investigate without a fourth agent.** Confirm that the candidate has been presented to the two affected live holders before starting the window. The diagnosis role checks the candidate. The recovery role can continue reversible work under its provisional plan, park that work or request a provisional fork, stating the dependency and consequence. The independent export continues. Investigation is bounded to five completed actions or 90 seconds. A speculative fork shares its role's limits and takes no additional model slot.
4. **Evidence becomes available.** At the end of that window, the fixture exposes the historical trace and applied policy for the exact incident. This releases information; it does not itself verify anything. A deterministic owner checker replays the captured workload against the captured code and applied configuration and performs the pre-registered cause-isolation probes. A valid result supports or contradicts P0. Wrong event IDs, moved/missing inputs, ambiguous simultaneous causes or failed replay remain inconclusive.
5. **Resolve.** The operator publishes the same qualified evidence/result in every arm. A preserves supported work and closes the capacity investigation. B invalidates P0. Affected holders stop before further write actions, preserve their abandoned commits and replan. If a write is already in flight, request interruption at the first safe boundary and record whether it completed; do not claim retroactive prevention. Fresh-context arms replace only the affected sessions after their prescribed trigger. Same-conversation Git changes its plan inside the existing session. Record the delay until recognition, stop, handoff and first correct action separately. No group advances to follow-up workloads until its resolution treatment and delivery evidence are recorded.
6. **Exercise the new understanding.** Give every role three short follow-up requests, in the same surviving conversation or replacement session. They exercise an affordable burst under the applied capacity, a genuinely over-budget request, and final reporting/export. Neither the wording nor the owner supplies a repair algorithm. These requests test whether the old diagnosis continues to shape later work.
7. **Finish on the shared product.** Wait for all submitted landings or the run deadline, then evaluate a fresh checkout of an exact shared SHA. Missing/unfinished claims do not pass. Preserve every intermediate result and outstanding investigation.

If evidence is inconclusive, it stays inconclusive: the default parks only the work explicitly betting on that uncertainty after the window. It never verifies a suspicion, creates a new reality automatically or stops the independent task. A warning alone does not require abandoning A. A confirmed contradiction does not authorize wholesale deletion of budget code.

## Verification and a fair fresh handoff

Verification binds `incident_id`, workload digest, code SHA at the incident, applied configuration revision, trace digest and verifier version. It establishes the cause of that historical incident, not a universal diagnosis of every later failure. Current machine settings are insufficient historical evidence. Changes made after the incident do not rewrite its trace. Claims needing a claim about the current deployment require a separate check against the current versions.

Before model runs, a no-model rehearsal must show that the checker distinguishes A and B, rejects the obsolete A clue, rejects the correct-incident/wrong-revision trace, and remains inconclusive on ambiguous replay. It also tests both faults as an oracle challenge, without adding a real-agent arm. The independently correct product passes; raising/removing the budget, mislabelling capacity as cost exhaustion, dropping affordable work, duplicating effects and corrupting CSV fail acceptance.

The replacement brief is a fixed projection of the record: public promises, current task/scope, accepted code SHA, surviving assumptions, checked current facts and source references, and remaining work. It contains no abandoned-plan transcript or operator-authored solution. Git-fresh and Superpose-fresh use the same projection and content limits. Preserve the full discarded reasoning in the record outside the new prompt. Retain independently validated code; retire only code/claims that actually require the disproved premise. Revocation, code selection and session start are separate timestamped operations. Replacement requires a checked contradiction of a premise the holder still relies on; unverified or refuted reset requests are recorded and declined under the same policy in both fresh-session arms.

After B, count any later action or new claim justified by P0. Merely naming the old hypothesis as rejected history is not continued reliance. Test behavior on the follow-up workloads; a fluent acknowledgment of the correction is not successful recovery.

## Proposed pilot limits and execution gates

Two worlds × three arms = six runs, one episode per cell, three live coding slots per run. Eighteen initial role sessions plus at most four affected-role replacements in B = at most twenty-two sessions. No new agent during investigation. The fixed order is A/Git-same, B/Superpose, A/Git-fresh, B/Git-fresh, A/Superpose, B/Git-same. It alternates worlds and reverses arm order; it does not eliminate order effects with only one block. Agent-visible workspace names are opaque episode labels. A world-code failure repeats only as a separately labelled instrumentation episode and remains in the ledger.

Each role has a shared maximum of 50 counted tool actions, 15 minutes elapsed from its first model turn and $0.50 in conservative standard-tier API-equivalent usage across its original session, investigation and any replacement. The role clock includes pauses and waits; a restart cannot reset any limit. The six-run ceiling is $9 in that accounting measure, not a subscription invoice. Actual billing stays unknown where the provider gives no dollars. Each run has a 20-minute deadline including setup, waits and final evaluation; the maximum scheduled six-run wall time is two hours. No semantic retry, extra role, second block or silent budget extension.

Before launch: qualify inherited fixtures/oracle without a model; prove container/Git/knowledge isolation; implement the incident trace and version checks; measure explicit candidate publication and each delivery stage; implement selective interruption/lease revocation and fresh briefs; enforce limits across restarts; distinguish action limit, usage limit, timeout, manual stop, admission wait and provider errors; test cancellation and wait-for-landing evaluation. Verify that the container supplies every executable used by its installed hooks, and that independent checks/owner responses finish within their declared windows. The end-to-end coverage report must show all required A/B paths before any live comparison starts. Fix and rehearse the proposal-source/owner-adoption workflow separately as a regression gate; this experiment does not claim to test spontaneous article creation. Capture a source snapshot as well as hashes. Existing infrastructure provides much of the transport, but automatic session replacement and external-evidence provenance are not demonstrated features yet.
